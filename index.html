Platforms I’ve Led

NeoRegSeaX — Vessel Registration Platform Maritime

NeoRegSeaX is a custom-built, microservices-based vessel registration system developed for the St. Kitts & Nevis International Ship Registry. It digitizes the full ship registration lifecycle — from initial profile intake through regulatory (RP) verification, certificate issuance, and ongoing fleet management — replacing manual, paper-driven registry processes.

As Technical Project Consultant, I partnered with stakeholders to evaluate requirements and set the platform’s strategic direction, leading a product roadmap aligned to business goals and regulatory compliance, and defined the roadmap for AI-integrated capabilities (OCR/NLP document intake, anomaly detection for compliance risk, chatbot-assisted application support). This reflects my contribution to the platform’s direction and delivery roadmap on an initiative that continues to evolve.

Spring Boot microservices · React/TypeScript micro-frontends (Module Federation) · RabbitMQ event-driven architecture · PostgreSQL · AWS

Registration Workflow

7-state lifecycle from Draft through Provisional and Permanent Registration, with regulatory (RP01–RP40) checklist verification and deficiency tracking built in.

Event-Driven Architecture

25+ typed domain events across vessel, certificate, document, user and notification services via RabbitMQ, enabling real-time cross-service notifications.

AI-Ready Roadmap
